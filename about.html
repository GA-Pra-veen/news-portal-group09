About the portal

Welcome to RUHUNA News Portal! and we are dedicated to delivering timely, accurate and engaging coverage of news about events,academic events and students life across the university.

OUR PURPOSE

Bringing the Ruhuna community closer.

Ruhuna News Portal is a student-developed website concept created to bring university-related news, announcements and events together in one accessible place.

The portal aims to make it easier for students, staff and visitors to discover useful information about university life.
