Mission and Scope

Welcome to University News Portal! We are Group 09 and we are dedicated to delivering timely, accurate and engaging coverage of news about events,academic events and students life across the university.

Our goal is, to keep students,faculty and the community members informed while providing an open platform for creative storytelling.
